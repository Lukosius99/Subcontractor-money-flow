# Monthly Money Flow

register: product

## Product Purpose
Internal finance-control tool tracking monthly subcontractor money flow per project and object. Imports monthly Excel/Dynamics data and compares invoiced amounts against contracted amounts.

## Users
- Finance and project-control staff reviewing monthly subcontractor spend at office desks (daylight, large monitors, Excel side-by-side).
- Project managers checking object-level contract usage.
- Engineers validating imported monthly rows.

## Tone & Brand
Calm, trustworthy, data-first. A professional finance register: dense but legible, never flashy. Light theme. Blue accent for navigation/selection only; green/amber/red strictly as data status signals.

## Anti-references
- Consumer SaaS marketing gloss, gradients, glassmorphism.
- Dark "developer tool" dashboards.
- Sparse card grids that hide data behind clicks.

## Strategic principles
- Every number is comparable: tabular numerals, right-aligned money, consistent units (EUR).
- Status is glanceable: pills and usage bars carry the same color vocabulary everywhere.
- Detail on demand: summary table stays compact; monthly breakdowns live in a side drawer; audit data in collapsible sections.
