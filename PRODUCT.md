# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal department users working on company-managed desktop browsers. They inspect project and object-level subcontractor money flow, investigate warnings, and make a small number of trusted LAN-only corrections.

## Product Purpose

Turn SharePoint and Power Automate exports into a dependable monthly financial inspection tool. Success means users can understand project totals, trace source rows, identify missing contracts, and act without spreadsheet hunting or ambiguous figures.

## Brand Personality

Professional, restrained, operational. The interface should feel calm and trustworthy while keeping dense financial data easy to scan.

## Anti-references

Do not make the product resemble a public SaaS marketing dashboard, a decorative finance concept, or an oversized mobile-first card feed. Avoid novelty that competes with tables, amounts, status, and audit context.

## Design Principles

1. Financial values must remain legible and never collide, truncate silently, or depend on one viewport width.
2. Responsive behavior follows the available workspace, including the reduced width created by inspectors and drawers.
3. Preserve familiar tables, tabs, controls, and status language so experienced users can work quickly.
4. Use color only for selection, action, and meaningful status.
5. Keep source context and corrective actions close to the figures they explain.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard focus visibility, semantic labels, reduced-motion support, sufficient contrast, and layouts that remain usable under browser zoom and narrower viewports. Do not rely on color alone to communicate status.
